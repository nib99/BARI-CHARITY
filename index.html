About Us

Bari Charity Organisation (Dhaabbata Tola Ooltummaa Barii / المؤسسة الخيرية بري) was recently established by our brothers who are driven by deep human compassion and faith. After fulfilling all necessary legal requirements and permissions, the organisation was founded to serve the community with sincerity and purpose.

We are a non-profit charitable organisation committed to upholding the values of faith, compassion, and mutual support. Our work focuses on supporting the most vulnerable members of society — orphans, students of knowledge, and those facing hardship.

Our Mission & Vision

☪

Uphold the Faith

To strengthen and support the practice and preservation of religious values and knowledge in the community.

🧒

Support Orphans

Provide essential equipment, materials, and stand firmly beside orphaned children in their difficulties.

🤝

Help the Needy

Offer assistance and relief to those facing poverty, hardship, and crisis in our communities.

📚

Educational Support

Support religious students and Academy learners so that financial difficulties never interrupt their education.

❤️

Charitable Works

Engage in comprehensive acts of charity (tola ooltummaa) that uplift individuals and strengthen society for future generations.

Our Programs

Orphan Support

Providing essential materials, care items, and ongoing support to orphaned children so they can grow with dignity and hope.

Education & Academy Support

Ensuring students of knowledge and Academy learners continue their studies without interruption due to financial challenges.

Emergency & Needy Relief

Rapid assistance to individuals and families facing urgent hardship, poverty, or crisis situations.

Faith & Community Strengthening

Initiatives that uphold religious education, values, and community cohesion rooted in faith and mutual care.
